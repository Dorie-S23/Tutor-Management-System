# User Feedback — Sprint 3

The second round of user feedback, collected at the end of the Sprint 3 build. For the previous round, see [User Feedback — Sprint 2](./user-feedback.mdx).

## How the feedback was collected

Feedback was gathered on **29 September 2026** using a second **Google Form**, circulated to classmates and testers as in [Sprint 2](./user-feedback.mdx) (the questions were redrafted for this round — see the [Sprint 3 meeting minutes](./meeting-minutes-sprint-3.mdx)). The Sprint 2 questions on sign-up, look and feel, ease of use, adoption and the guided tutorial were kept, and the form added **task-based questions**: testers were asked to actually create a course, log hours for a session, view their timesheet and dispute an entry, import their timetable, and check which courses still need volunteers.

**9 responses** were received from **9 unique respondents**: 4 tested as **student volunteers**, 3 as **tutors** and 2 as **organisers**. No educator responses were collected this round, so the educator gaps raised in Sprint 2 remain untested. The raw responses remain in the form's linked Google Sheet; this page reports the findings.

## Findings by question

### Sign-up / log-in — a clean sweep again

All nine responses rated the experience "Great" (4) or "Good" (5), across volunteers, tutors and organisers — the second consecutive round with a 100% positive rating on this question.

### Look and feel — positive, with dark mode the recurring caveat

Five testers answered a flat yes; four qualified it:

> "The app looks really good. The design for certain pages looks really cool. Just would say that the themes of certain pages need to be considered. As majority of it is black and green. While another page is more multi-coloured. Which I like. But functionality is perfect." — Tutor

> "I would prefer that the dark mode feature have a better visibility." — Organiser

> "It looks good, just the font is too small" — Student volunteer

One tutor noted the trajectory from Sprint 2: "Yes, I dramatic improvement from last time."

Three testers in total raised **dark mode** across two questions — poor contrast ("On light mode, yes. On dark mode no." — Tutor, asked about colour and contrast) and odd styling on the sign-up page ("the dark mode is weird on sign up" — Student volunteer). The volunteer pages received a scoped dark-mode readability fix late in the sprint; these complaints point at the pages outside that fix's scope.

### Ease of use — up on Sprint 2

3 × "Very easy", 5 × "Easy", 1 × "Okay" — no negative ratings this round, where Sprint 2 included a "Difficult" and a navigation complaint. Only one tester mentioned anything they couldn't find — a student volunteer answered "Search bar" — while a tutor volunteered: "No, the dashboard is quite informative in the location of stuff."

### Would you use it at school — 8 of 9, but with real caveats

The unqualified yeses included "Yes because it's easy to interact with" and "yes i would use it at school because it's efficient" (both Student volunteers). One tutor's yes carried the round's sharpest workflow criticism:

> "Yes, the current tutor claim system leaves a lot to be desired. Tutors struggle to submit claim forms, and communication between all sides is difficult." — Tutor

And the single no was close to a yes:

> "No. It still has a few performance issues, but its close." — Tutor

### The guided tutorial — helpful, and last sprint's bug is confirmed fixed

All nine testers found the tutorial helpful. Sprint 2's complaint that the tour let users scroll past their steps is explicitly confirmed fixed:

> "No, I like that the devs fixed the tour and locked them in one place." — Organiser, asked whether the tutorial ever got in the way

Two testers flagged steps the tour should still add — the notifications bell ("it was useful, but it didn't show what the bell icon was" — Organiser) and the help icon ("yes it was good, it missed the icon with the question mark though" — Student volunteer). One tutor saw the tooltip follow them around after choosing to ignore the tour, but had "no complaints regarding it".

### What testers loved — "something we shouldn't change"

A new question this round; the answers clustered on the Sprint 3 features:

- "Loved the calendar and timesheet." — Tutor
- "the timetable parser and log hours" — Student volunteer
- "the timetable" — Organiser
- "History feature, it makes it easy to track past work" — Organiser
- "Layout and colour contrast" — Student volunteer
- "Submitting transcript" — Student volunteer
- "Time it took" — Student volunteer, on how quick tasks were to complete

Items 2–4 share a root cause: the organiser permission model reworked this sprint (all courses visible, course creation and auto-allocation restricted to the super-organiser, per-course access requests). The behaviour is intended; the UI just doesn't spell that out. Items 1, 8 and 9 continue Sprint 2's visual-polish and accessibility threads; item 7 is new.
