# User Feedback

## How the feedback was collected

Feedback was gathered using a **Google Form** — the ["Tutor Management System feedback form"](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc1J6JfxXUhNInpMYqt0qXJYzphPXVDOotBT0DWXgoheT2Xhw/viewform?usp=publish-editor) — circulated to classmates and testers. Responses were collected automatically into a [linked Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1euuLzB13hXVDXXdQYsXNAinAWlO4vB0msDuHh7Rfi1k/edit?usp=sharing) between 12 and 14 September 2026, just after the Milestone 2 build.

**11 responses** were received from **7 unique respondents**. One respondent completed the form once per role, so **all four roles** (organiser, tutor, student volunteer, educator) are represented. The raw responses remain in the linked sheet; this page reports the findings.

## Summary

| Question                                     | Positive                    | Mixed / qualified                       | Negative |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------- |
| How was signing up / logging in?             | 11 (8 "Great", 3 "Good")    | —                                       | —        |
| Does the app look good?                      | 10                          | —                                       | 1        |
| Is the guided onboarding (tutorial) helpful? | 9                           | 1 ("There's no tutorial for this role") | —        |
| How easy is the app to use?                  | 8 (5 "Very easy", 3 "Easy") | 2 ("Okay")                              | 1        |
| Would you use this system at school?         | 9 (incl. 1 enthusiastic)    | 1 ("Not sure")                          | 1        |

Headline results: sign-up/log-in is a clean sweep (**11/11 positive**), and **9/11 testers would happily adopt the system at school**. The gaps cluster in three places: the **Educator role** (no tutorial, dashboard seen as incomplete), **navigation intuitiveness** for at least one tester, and **tutorial polish** (scroll behaviour and tip ordering).

## Findings by question

### Sign-up / log-in — universally positive

All 11 responses rated the experience "Great" (8) or "Good" (3), across every role — covering both email/password and Google OAuth registration.

### Look and feel — positive, with one outlier

Nine testers answered a flat "Yes"; one qualified it:

> "Overall it looks great and professional … a bit more colour could help some things pop" — Lungile Shezi (Student Volunteer)

The single negative came from the Educator walkthrough:

> "It looks trivial and incomplete. Nothing to do here" — Clementinah (Educator)

### Guided onboarding — helpful everywhere it exists

All nine responses from roles that have a tutorial (organiser, tutor, student volunteer) found it helpful — two with constructive suggestions (below). The tenth had nothing to evaluate:

> "There's no tutorial for this role" — Clementinah (Educator)

### Ease of use — mostly easy

5 × "Very easy", 3 × "Easy", 2 × "Okay", 1 × "Difficult". The one "Difficult" carried the strongest criticism in the survey:

> "The user experience is not on par as yet. I wasn't able to navigate the system in [a way] that was intuitive and easy to navigate." — Mandla Msibi (Tutor)

### Would you use it at school — a strong yes

Nine yeses, including one standout:

> "Spectacular, give me 14 of them right now ☝️" — Khyati (Tutor)

…and one conditional:

> "As a hypothetical tutor, I would use this app." — Lungile Shezi (Student Volunteer)

The "Not sure" and the navigation complaint above both trace back to the Educator experience and overall navigation respectively.
